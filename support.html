Support

A question, a bug, a wrong postal code? Write to support@tecknologiks.com. Tell us your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version if something goes wrong.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my Premium purchase?

Open the Premium screen (tap “Ad-free” under the banner, or the plane button at the top left of the Favorites tab) and tap “Restore purchases”, signed in with the same Apple Account as for the purchase. Premium also restores on your other devices signed in with that account.

How do I change or withdraw my ad consent?

On the Premium screen, tap “Privacy options” to open the consent form again. The button appears only where Google’s consent platform requires it (in particular in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland). To turn off ad tracking, go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See also “Changing your choices” in the privacy policy.

“Around me” says Location is off

Allow Code Postal to use your location: tap “Open Settings” in the app, or go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Code Postal. Searching by name or postal code works without it. If your position cannot be determined, check that Location Services are on, then try again.

A commune, a postal code or a figure looks wrong

The commune data is built into the app from public sources (La Poste, INSEE, Géo API) and is updated with new versions of the app. To report an error, write to support@tecknologiks.com with the commune name, its INSEE code if you know it, and what you expected.

Where are my favorites?

Favorites and history are stored only on your device. They are not synchronised and not transferred to a new device.
