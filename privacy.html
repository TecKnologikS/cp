Privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

In short: Code Postal has no account and no server of its own. Your favorites and history stay on your device, and the commune data is built into the app. The free version shows one ad banner through Google AdMob, which collects data to serve and measure ads, and personalises them only if you agree; Premium users see no ad and no advertising SDK is started for them. The “Around me” tab uses your position only when you open it, does not keep it, and, when online, sends it to the French government’s Géo API to confirm the commune.

1. Who is responsible

Code Postal and this website are published by TecKnologikS, data controller under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact for any question about your data: support@tecknologiks.com.

2. Data stored on your device

Your favorites and your history of viewed communes are stored only on your device, in the app. There is no account, and TecKnologikS has no server that receives them. You can clear the history from the Favorites screen; deleting the app erases everything.

3. Commune data

The commune data (names, postal codes, INSEE codes, population, area, altitude, departments, regions) is embedded in the app: searching and exploring work without sending anything. It comes from public sources: the postal code database of La Poste, the official geographic code (COG) of INSEE and the Géo API.

4. Location (“Around me” tab)

  • The app asks for permission to use your location while you use it. It is used only to find your commune and its postal code.
  • Your position is requested once when you open the “Around me” tab, and again if you tap “Locate again”. The app does not track you continuously.
  • The position is not stored. A first answer is computed on your device from the embedded data.
  • When you are online, your position (latitude and longitude) is then sent to the Géo API of the French State (geo.api.gouv.fr) to confirm the commune and its postal code, and the service receives the technical data of any web request, such as your IP address. If you are offline, nothing is sent. No other service receives your position from the app.
  • You can refuse or withdraw the permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Code Postal. Search by name or postal code keeps working.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a single banner, in the Search tab, provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Premium users see no ad, and for them the consent step and the advertising SDK are not started. The SDK collects the following data:

This data may be used to track you across apps and websites of other companies, only with your permission (see below). Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google’s privacy policy.

Your consent

  • Where Google’s consent platform requires it (in particular the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) is shown at launch. You can accept, refuse or choose by purpose. If you refuse, ads may still be shown, without personalisation.
  • On iOS, once ads are allowed, the system asks whether the app may use your advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency). If you refuse, the advertising identifier is not shared.

6. Premium purchase

Premium is a one-time purchase handled by Apple (App Store, StoreKit). The payment is processed by Apple under its own privacy policy: the app and TecKnologikS receive no payment data. The app only checks with the App Store whether Premium is active on your Apple Account.

8. Legal bases

  • Your consent: personalised ads, the advertising identifier, storing or reading information on your device for advertising, and the use of your position.
  • Our legitimate interest: showing non-personalised ads that keep the app free, and measuring and protecting them against fraud.
  • The performance of the contract: handling your Premium purchase through Apple.

9. How long

  • Favorites and history: on your device, until you remove them or delete the app.
  • Position: not kept by the app.
  • Advertising data: kept by Google under its own retention rules.
  • Data received by the Géo API or by Apple: kept under their own rules.

10. Changing your choices and deleting your data

  • Consent for ads: open the Premium screen (tap “Ad-free” under the banner, or the plane button at the top left of the Favorites tab), then tap “Privacy options” to open the consent form again and change or withdraw your choice at any time. This button appears only where Google’s consent platform requires it, and only in the free version.
  • Advertising identifier: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, then turn off Code Postal.
  • Location: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Code Postal.
  • Favorites and history: remove them in the app, or delete the app.

TecKnologikS has no account and no user database, so there is nothing else to delete on our side.

11. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, object to, restrict and port your data, and to withdraw your consent. To exercise them, write to support@tecknologiks.com. For data held by Google, Apple or the Géo API, you can also contact them directly. You can lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: www.cnil.fr).

12. Children

TecKnologikS does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Parents can turn off tracking in iOS Settings and refuse personalised ads in the consent form.

13. This website

This website sets no cookies and loads no third-party resources.

14. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page will be updated.
